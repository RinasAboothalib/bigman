About Big Man & Co

Big food.No shortcuts.

A restaurant on Chaandhanee Magu in Malé, with a Delivery House in Hulhumalé. Premium quality since 2017.

The house

It’s goodto see you!

That is the first line on our menu, and we mean it. Big Man serves grilled burgers and a sandwich with its own name branded into the bread.

You can eat in or take away at Chaandhanee Magu, or have it brought to you from the BM Delivery House in Hulhumalé. Either way the menu works the same: pick your plate, pick your protein, pick your style.

Alongside the burgers and subs you will find local favourites: Valho King over rice, rotti, pasta or noodles, and the Keyolhu! nachos with valhomas and rihaakuru cheese.
