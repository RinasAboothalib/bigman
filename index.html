About Big Man & Co

It’s goodto see you!

That is the first line on our menu, and we mean it. Big Man is a restaurant on Chaandhanee Magu in Malé, with a Delivery House in Hulhumalé.

The burgers are grilled, never anything else, and the meat is 100% meat, always fresh. Build your own in six styles from Bigman to Ghost Pepper, or go straight for the Manwich, the king of sandwiches.

Beyond the bun there are Rockets jumbo subs, W.T.F loaded fries and nachos, pasta, wok plates and kebabs, plus local favourites like Valho King and the Keyolhu! nachos with valhomas and rihaakuru cheese.
